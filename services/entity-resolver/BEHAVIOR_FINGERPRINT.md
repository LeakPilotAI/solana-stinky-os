# Behavioral Fingerprint Evidence Contract

Genesis behavioral fingerprints are descriptive summaries of observed entity launch history.

They may describe launch cadence and measured outcome coverage, but they do not assign quality, risk, prediction, trade direction, or position sizing.

Missing outcomes remain unknown. A new entity with insufficient history remains observationally unknown.

The fingerprint is recomputed from `entity_launches`, so the stored summary is derived from persistent evidence rather than mutable counters alone.
